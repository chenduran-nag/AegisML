What a run does
  1. Profile. Every column is summarised. Identifier and constant columns are dropped, distribution issues go to the planner, and suspected leakage or proxy variables are held for you.
  2. Plan. The LLM sees aggregate statistics only and proposes preprocessing and models. It never touches the data.
  3. Clean and split. Train, validation and test rows are separated before anything is fitted.
  4. Train and audit. Models are ranked on the validation rows, and fairness is measured on protected attributes.
  5. Decide. The run stops for your review. Approving saves the model, scores it once on the untouched test rows and writes its model card.

Every step is written to a hash-chained audit log, under the rules in policy.yaml.
